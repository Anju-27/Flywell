# Flywell Manpower Consultants

A professional and responsive website for **Flywell Manpower Consultants**, a manpower recruitment and consultancy company providing comprehensive and cost-effective staffing solutions for businesses and industries, with a major presence across the Middle East.

## 🌐 About the Project

Flywell Manpower Consultants helps businesses connect with suitable manpower and recruitment solutions based on their workforce requirements.

This website is designed to present the company's services, career opportunities, company information, and contact details in a clean, professional, and user-friendly interface.

## ✨ Features

- 🏠 Professional Home Page
- 👨‍💼 About Company Section
- 💼 Manpower & Recruitment Services
- 📋 Career Opportunities
- 📞 Contact Page
- 📱 Responsive Design
- 🎨 Clean and Professional UI
- 🌍 Middle East Manpower Consultancy Focus
- ⚡ Easy Navigation
- 📄 Multiple Website Pages

## 📄 Website Pages

- **Home** – Introduction and overview of Flywell Manpower Consultants
- **About** – Information about the company
- **Services** – Recruitment and manpower consultancy services
- **Career** – Career and job-related information
- **Contact** – Contact and enquiry information

- PHP
